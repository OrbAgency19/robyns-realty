Home Buying Process

  1. Select a Realtor
  2. Obtain Financial Pre-approval with Mortgage or Loan Officer
  3. Analyze your Needs in a Buyer Consultation
  4. Select Properties: your Realtor will create a personal account on Utah MLS
  5. View Properties with your agent and/or at Open Houses
  6. Agent Writes up an Offer to Purchase, and any addenda required
  7. Negotiate Terms (dates, price, HOA fees, earnest money, down-payment)
  8. Acceptance of the Contract
  9. Turn in “earnest money” to your Agent's Brokerage (within 4 days of contract)
  10. Remove Contingencies: Verify this is the Right Property
  11. Due Diligence: Inspection, Resolve any issues (1st two weeks)
  12. Financing: Solidify loan, underwriting, documentation (up to 3 weeks)
  13. Get Appraisal, Obtain Survey and Arrange Home Insurance
  14. Conduct Title Search, Remove Encumbrances, Obtain Title Insurance
  15. Settlement: Signing all loan and title documents (1 month +/- from offer)
  16. Loan is Funded: Seller obtains Buyer's funds for Closing (within 24 hrs)
  17. Ensure Recording of New Property Title (up to 24 hours post signing docs)
  18. Get keys and take Possession of Home
