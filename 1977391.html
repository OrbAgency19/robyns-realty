Discover the epitome of luxury living with this stunning Herriman, Rosecrest custom home built by a semi-custom builder. Boasting a generous floor plan, this residence features a total of 7 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms, providing ample space for both family and guests.

The soaring cathedral ceilings on the main floor in the great room add to spacious feeling. On the main level, you'll find the Owner's Suite with custom wood work in the bedroom, a jacuzzi tub and separate shower. Two more bedrooms with a full bath are located on the other wing of the home. A den/office or formal living room, plus main floor laundry round out the main level. Every window has white plantation shutters. Upstairs is an oversized loft to be utilized for any purpose you like. The lower level of the home boasts a convenient basement apartment, offering a private retreat for guests or potential rental income. This versatile space adds an extra layer of functionality to the property, enhancing its value and appeal. Homes runs on a new tankless water heater. *Add a walk out entrance to complete it as an ideal future rental space. The 4 car garage (2 door and 1 tandem entrance) and built in storage above the car area will delight any garage-lover. Every inch of this home has been meticulously kept and adorned to showcase a seamless blend of simple elegance and timeless charm. From the moment you step through the front door, you'll be greeted by an atmosphere of sophistication and warmth. Marvel at the breathtaking mountain views that surround the property, creating a picturesque backdrop for your daily life. Don't miss the opportunity to make this gorgeous custom home your own. Immerse yourself in the luxury of spacious living, breathtaking views, and the convenience of multiple kitchens. This property is not just a home; it's a lifestyle waiting to be embraced.
