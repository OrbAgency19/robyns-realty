Selling a Home?

Here's what I'll do to make it a smooth, profitable experience

  1. Detailed Market Analysis, local pricing and sales in past 4 months
  2. Staging consultation: Detailed review of each room in the home
  3. Offer services of professional stager or housecleaners if preferred
  4. Offer free staging items and service if smaller items are required
  5. Hire a professional Home Sales Photographer
  6. Arrange online photo tour set to music, with panoramic & static photos
  7. Market your home on hundreds of websites, including social media sites
  8. Set up all showings; coordinate with agents and buyers
  9. Request feedback from every visitor to your home
  10. Secure your home with a mechanical key box & require appts before show
  11. Provide flyers, via mail, email and in your home for showings
  12. Hold Open Houses as often as you would like them
  13. Negotiate offers: pricing, dates and included/excluded items in the offer
  14. Coordinate between title company, inspector, appraiser and loan officer of buying party, including buyer's realtor
  15. Keep you apprised of all deadlines and dates
  16. Legally protect you in the Real Estate Purchase Contract
  17. Walk you through the process with weekly contact, available 7 days/week
  18. Attend the Settlement (Closing) with you and your escrow officer
  19. Ensure timely transfer of keys and property to buyer
  20. Stay in touch with you: invitations to client events, future market analysis, etc.

Staging Your Home to Sell

First, consider what buyers are looking for?

  • S P A C E
  • Seeing their family and their stuff in your home
  • A welcoming feeling (including smells, sights and sounds)
  • Clean, bright, open, inviting

Recommended by Robyn and professional home stagers

  • Curb appeal: Bright colors in front: pillows, chairs, flowers, pots. No dead plants.
  • Front door and front of home: Clear cobwebs, dust, wipe down or re-paint your door. That is the first thing the buyer will see. Is the handle firm? Clean? Is door dingy or bright & clean?
  • Entry to home, once inside should create a sense of space, warmth and invitation
  • Clear all floors of objects, including closets, pantry & laundry
  • Pack up items that you can live without for 2-4 months: clothes, toys, shoes, books, etc.
  • Keep hallways and entry into all rooms clear from furniture
  • De-clutter items on counters, bookshelves & closet shelves. Can you pack it?
  • De-personalize: have your home look like a model home, beautiful & warm, not personal
  • Touch up paint; wipe down walls & baseboards, as well as doors and trim

Consider a few purchases to make your home feel NEW

  • Bath towels, hand-towels, kitchen towels (use just for showings)
  • Rugs (get rid of any that are dingy, worn, dull or stained)
  • Bedding (comforters, pillows)
  • Candle or plug ins (not strong ones!)

Anything you do purchase, get what you love, and take it to your NEW house
